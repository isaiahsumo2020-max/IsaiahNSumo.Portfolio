02 /  About

More than just a student —
a builder with purpose.

I'm a first-year senior student at BlueCrest University Liberia, studying Information Technology with a strong focus on Networking & System Administration. My technical foundation spans Linux/Windows fundamentals, subnetting, routing, and network troubleshooting.

Beyond the classroom, I advocate for Cybersecurity awareness and policy — promoting digital safety through youth empowerment initiatives and community education. I participated in YouthIGF2024 Liberia and attended Global Encryption Day, contributing to conversations shaping digital rights across Africa.

I'm the founder of LEADS Liberia (Liberia Enterprise for Advance Digital Solutions) and serve as President of the PGA Foundation (Passionate Goals Achievers). Through these platforms, I work to bring technology access and digital literacy to underserved communities.
